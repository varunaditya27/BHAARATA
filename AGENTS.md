# BHĀRATA frontend design contract

BHĀRATA is an editorial historical atlas, not a SaaS dashboard.

- Do not introduce purple gradients, glassmorphism, giant rounded cards, fake metrics or generic feature grids.
- The map, chronology, selected historical entity and evidence trail are primary interface objects.
- Prefer asymmetry, ruled divisions, typographic hierarchy, restrained color and information density.
- Use motion to explain spatial or temporal change, not as decoration.
- Keep important historical context visible without hover-only interactions.
- Historical prototype records must be labeled as such.
- Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion behavior and semantic headings.
- Avoid transition: all. Animate opacity and transform intentionally.
- Do not invent historical certainty. Approximate dates should remain approximate.

Voice: precise, curious, quietly confident. No startup jargon.
